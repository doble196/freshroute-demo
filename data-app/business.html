Who else does the city inspect?

A restaurant has to hang its grade in the window. A barbershop, a swimming pool, a daycare, a car wash and a tax preparer are all inspected too — the results are public, and almost nobody reads them. Pick a trade and look one up.

Reads the city's API live when you search. Nothing is stored.

Sources, read live: 43nn-pn8j restaurants · jzhd-m6uv DCWP inspections · 3kfa-rvez pools · dsg6-ifza childcare centres.

Different agencies publish different things: DOHMH gives restaurants a numeric score, DCWP records only whether a violation was issued, pools get violation counts, and childcare centres get a violation rate next to the city average. They are not comparable to each other and this page does not pretend they are.

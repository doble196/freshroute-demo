Pursuit demo day · the storyboard

You failed the inspection.
What do you fix first?

FreshRoute in five one-sentence beats — the person before the product, the product not named until Beat 4. Say each sentence aloud; rewrite any that don’t sound like you. The structure survives rewording.

The format’s law: one sentence per beat — a beat you can’t say in one sentence is a beat you haven’t decided yet. All five below pass the storyboard skill’s six gates.
  1. 1ROLE a person you could pick out of a room

    The operator of a bubble-tea shop on Main Street in Flushing failed their health inspection five days ago, and tonight they are deciding what their crew fixes before a re-inspection they cannot schedule.

  2. 2PROBLEM the pain and its root cause, together

    They have nine violations and 41 points but no idea where to start, because the city publishes each citation as a line on a list and never tells you which violations actually come back at the re-inspection.

  3. 3VALUE an outcome they’d notice by Friday

    Instead of splitting a week of crew time nine ways, they spend it on the three violations that citywide stay unfixed at the re-inspection most often — 53%, 43%, and 41% of the time.

  4. 4SOLUTION the flow, verbs in order — the product’s first appearance

    They open FreshRoute’s inspection page, type the shop’s name, pick their address from the five that share it, and read their own city record ranked by how often each violation survived the re-inspection.

  5. 5DEMO what the room should watch happen

    I’ll run that exact lookup live on the city’s data right now — when the record loads, watch the ranking appear with the mice violation everyone fears sitting third, under two items nobody in this room would have fixed first.

Run the demo

  • Type moge tee → Enter. Pick 42-35 Main Street · Queens.
  • When the banner lands, stop talking — let the room read “3 of your last 3 inspections” themselves.
  • The numbers stay on screen, the meaning stays in your mouth — never read the n and the interval aloud.
  • Backup subject: kingston pizza → the Kingston Avenue · Brooklyn row.
  • If anything breaks: ten seconds of honest diagnosis, one pivot sentence, then the fallback captures. The error state is a feature — say so.

Checked, not vibes

  • All six storyboard gates pass on these exact sentences: five beats, one sentence each, no semicolon splices, product waits until Beat 4, a cause in Beat 2, a watchable event in Beat 5.
  • The checker earned its keep: the first Beat 5 draft said “land third” — a state, not an event — and the demo_visible gate rejected it. “Watch the ranking appear” is both compliant and better stagecraft.
  • The persistence numbers are measured, not vibes either: 6,960 paired NYC inspections, computed offline, embedded in the page — the demo can’t un-measure them.

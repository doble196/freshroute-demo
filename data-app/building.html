What does the building say?

A restaurant's grade describes the restaurant. These records describe the building it sits in — city rodent inspections and public complaints at the same tax lot. Useful context, and only that: nearly half of NYC restaurants share a lot with another restaurant, so these records may belong to a neighbour.

Reads the city's API live when you search. Nothing is stored.

Sources, all NYC OpenData, read live at search time: 43nn-pn8j restaurant inspections · p937-wjvj rodent inspections · erm2-nwe9 311 service requests. Joined on bbl, the Borough-Block-Lot tax-lot identifier both datasets publish.

This page shows public records. It is not a rating, it does not feed a rating, and no business can change what appears here.
